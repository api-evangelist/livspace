# Livspace

> India's largest home interior design and renovation company. Founded 2014.
> Serves 125+ cities across India. End-to-end service: free design consultation
> → 3D visualisation → factory-made modular products → on-site installation.
> Lifetime warranty on modular products. 100+ Experience Centres for in-person consultation.
> Minimum project budget: ₹3.62 lakhs. Modular kitchen starting at ₹1.7 lakhs.
